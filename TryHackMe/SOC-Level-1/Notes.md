
**What I Learned: Blue Team & SOC Fundamentals**
This section covers the foundational concepts of defensive security and the operational structure of a Security Operations Center (SOC).

SOC Roles & Operations: Broke down the day-to-day responsibilities of a Tier 1 Security Analyst, including incident triage, monitoring workflows, and the general structure and career progression within a blue team.

The Human Attack Vector: Explored why people are consistently targeted through social engineering and phishing, and how a SOC actively monitors and defends against user-targeted attacks.

System Vulnerabilities & Defense: Looked at the defensive side of system exploitation, specifically how threat actors take advantage of misconfigurations and unpatched infrastructure, and the mitigation strategies needed to secure those environments.

Overall, this provided a clear perspective on how organizations actively monitor and defend their networks, offering a strong defensive contrast to standard vulnerability assessments and offensive operations.

I learned that my primary job as a first responder is triage—carefully investigating data to separate actual threats (True Positives) from everyday network noise (False Positives). **What I Learned: Core SOC Solutions**

Completing the **Core SOC Solutions** section helped me understand the actual tools SOC analysts rely on every day, not just the theory behind defense. It connected the bigger SOC workflow to real platforms used for detection, investigation, and response.

- **Introduction to EDR:** I learned how Endpoint Detection and Response tools monitor endpoint activity, detect suspicious behavior, and help analysts investigate threats directly on hosts. It made me realize how important endpoint visibility is during incident triage.

- **Introduction to SIEM:** This room gave me a solid understanding of how SIEM platforms collect and correlate logs from different sources to identify suspicious patterns. I now better understand why SIEM is central to SOC monitoring.

- **Splunk: The Basics:** I practiced how analysts search and investigate logs in Splunk. Learning how to query events and pivot through data made the investigation process feel much more practical and realistic.

- **Elastic Stack: The Basics:** I explored how ELK is used for log collection, analysis, and visualization. It showed me another common SOC workflow for handling large log volumes and spotting security-relevant activity.

- **Introduction to SOAR:** I learned how SOAR helps automate repetitive SOC tasks and improves incident response speed through orchestration and playbooks. This gave me perspective on how modern SOCs scale their operations efficiently.

Overall, this section gave me a clear understanding of how **EDR, SIEM, and SOAR** work together in a real SOC environment. It made the analyst role feel more operational and hands-on, and showed me how detection and response are supported by the right security stack.
